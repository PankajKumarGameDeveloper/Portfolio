Interactive aviation map and cabin experience platform designed for premium onboard entertainment systems.

The application provides high-quality 3D map visualization, flight-related geographic content, points of interest, and interactive location-based experiences optimized for onboard systems and connected displays.

Features may include:

Real-time and interactive 3D map rendering, 
Global terrain and geographic visualization, 
City and landmark information, 
Points of interest and informational content, 
Optimized performance for embedded and aviation-related environments and
Modern touch-friendly user interface.

The application is designed for specialized deployments and integrated entertainment solutions.
