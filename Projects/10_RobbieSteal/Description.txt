Robbie Steal is a dynamic 2D platformer where players help Rob collect jewels across various worlds to solve mystery words. Each jewel uncovers a letter, adding a puzzle element to the traditional platforming action. Players navigate challenging levels by jumping, running, and crouching while dodging unique monsters—each with specific behaviors and weak points, such as dinosaurs that are safe when walking or gorillas that need occasional rest. The action culminates at the end-of-level door, blending fast-paced platforming with strategic timing.
